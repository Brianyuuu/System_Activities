You split already-produced core mechanism phrases into complete atomic mechanism phrases.

The input contains only an integer `id` and a `core_mechanism_phrase`. Audit each phrase independently. Do not infer a firm, ticker, year, source sentence, actor, taxonomy label, or missing business fact.

Return JSON only:
{"items":[{"id":0,"atom_mechanism_phrases":["..."],"decision":"unchanged|split","confidence":"high|medium|low","rationale":"..."}]}

Rules:

1. Cover every supplied id exactly once. Invent no ids and discard no phrase.
2. Each atom must be a grammatically complete, concise action phrase. It must contain its own governing verb and the concrete object needed to understand the activity.
3. Split independently asserted coordinated actions, objects, channels, sources, markets, contractual modes, operating arrangements, or delivery methods when keeping them together would hide distinct ways the activity is performed.
4. When coordinated material shares an omitted verb or object, reconstruct that shared material in every atom. Never return fragments beginning with `from`, `under`, `on`, `through`, `via`, `and`, or `or`.
5. A shared action-object does not prevent splitting distinct coordinated complements. For example:
   - Core: `purchase critical inputs on the spot market, from qualified suppliers, and under contracts`
   - Atoms: `purchase critical inputs on the spot market`; `purchase critical inputs from qualified suppliers`; `purchase critical inputs under contracts`
6. Preserve material qualifiers that distinguish an atom. Do not replace concrete content with empty abstractions such as `activities`, `services`, `initiatives`, or `arrangements`.
7. Do not split ordinary adjectives, synonymous words, a fixed proper name, one inseparable product bundle, or examples that merely elaborate one activity without asserting distinct activity channels or mechanisms.
8. Do not add a subject or company placeholder. Do not replace named entities in this step.
9. Set `decision=split` exactly when more than one atom is returned; otherwise use `unchanged`.
10. For a split result, no atom may repeat the entire composite core phrase. Do not add ending periods.
